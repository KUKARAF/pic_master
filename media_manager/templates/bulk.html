👤 Faces

higher = fewer, more certain matches

Run this once after upgrading. Faces that were sideways or upside-down in the photo were detected with mis-aligned landmarks, so the face vector stored for them is scrambled — that is why such a face never matched the right person no matter how many times you confirmed them. This job re-opens each already-detected face, works out its in-plane rotation, and re-embeds it upright. It only touches faces stored before the fix (newly detected faces are already correct), and it changes no boxes, names or decisions — only the vectors used for matching. Re-run “🤝 Auto-match” (or face scoring on Review faces) afterwards so the repaired faces get re-ranked.

🎬 Videos

Saves real stills at 10/25/50/75/90% (reuses the frame-capture architecture); the perceptual-hash job then indexes them. A video whose frames won't decode is flagged damaged.

Samples each video at ~1 fps, then clusters faces across frames and captures a still only for a person who recurs across several frames (using their best/sharpest frame) — so one-frame and tiny/blurry background faces don't become junk matches. Uses the match confidence above; ≈ one still per distinct person, capped per video. Skips videos already scanned.
